# Returns and exchanges
You have 30 days from the delivery date to start a return. Open the order in your account, choose "Return or exchange", and print the prepaid label.

Items must be unused and in their original packaging. Exchanges ship as soon as the carrier scans your return. Return shipping is free for damaged or incorrect items; otherwise a flat label fee is deducted from your refund.

Digital goods and gift cards cannot be returned.
