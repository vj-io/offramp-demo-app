# Shipping times
Orders leave our warehouses within 1 business day. Delivery estimates after dispatch: domestic standard 3 to 5 business days, domestic express 1 to 2 business days, EU 4 to 7 business days, rest of world 7 to 14 business days.

Orders to Switzerland, Norway and the UK may be held at customs, which can add 2 to 5 days. Business days exclude weekends and public holidays at the destination.
