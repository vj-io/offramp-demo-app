# Seat pricing and volume discounts
Plans are priced per seat per billing period. Seats are billed in graduated tiers: seats 1 to 10 pay the full list price, seats 11 to 50 pay 85% of list price, and seats 51 and above pay 70%.

A tier discount applies only to the seats inside that tier, not to every seat. Coupons are applied to the total after the tier discounts.
