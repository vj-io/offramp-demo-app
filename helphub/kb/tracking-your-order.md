# Tracking your order
Once your order ships you receive an email with a tracking link and the carrier name. You can also find it under "Orders" in your account.

Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show its first scan. If a parcel has not moved for 5 business days, contact us with the order id and we will open a trace with the carrier. Parcels not delivered 21 days after dispatch are treated as lost and reshipped or refunded.
