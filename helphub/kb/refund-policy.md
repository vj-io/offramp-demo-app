# Refund policy
Physical orders can be refunded within 30 days of delivery if the item is unused, damaged, or not as described. Refunds go back to the original payment method and usually appear within 5 to 10 business days.

Subscriptions are refunded separately. Annual plans cancelled within 14 days of the billing period start receive a full refund. After that we refund the unused part of the period, prorated by day, minus a 3% processing fee. Monthly plans are prorated by day from the first day.

Refunds are issued in the currency you paid in, at the exchange rate recorded when you paid.
