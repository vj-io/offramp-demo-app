# Payment methods and billing details
We accept major credit and debit cards, PayPal, and SEPA direct debit in the EU. Update your card or billing address under Account, Billing. Changes apply from the next charge.

A failed payment is retried automatically after 3 days and again after 7 days. If both retries fail the subscription is paused, not deleted, and you can reactivate it by updating your payment method.
